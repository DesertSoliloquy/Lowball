Put the card pictures in this folder.

Names (all lowercase, .webp):
  card_3d_01.webp   three of diamonds, first picture
  card_ac_08.webp   eighth Ace of Clubs
  joker_01.webp     a joker
  back_01.webp      a card back

Ranks: a 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 t j q k
Suits: c (clubs) d (diamonds) h (hearts) s (spades)

Any card without a picture is drawn as a plain card, so the game works before
the pictures are added.
